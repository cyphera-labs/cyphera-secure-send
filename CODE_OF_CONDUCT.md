# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project, in issues, pull requests, and
discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive. Disagree with
ideas, not with people. Assume good faith. Keep the conversation about the
work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
remarks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to conduct@cyphera.io. Reports are handled privately by the
maintainers. Outcomes range from a private note to removal from the project's
spaces, in proportion to the behavior.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available
at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
